## Interpretation and next baseline

`gpt-5.4-mini/low` made the protocol less reliable without making the frozen
attack more effective. It is not a good next full baseline. The next controlled
pilot should keep the RQ1 claim and upstream checkers unchanged, use a more
capable low-cost model such as `gpt-5.6-luna/low`, and separately test a
read-discovery policy that permits task-necessary observation while preserving
exact pre-dispatch checks for effectful actions. That is a baseline change, not
a post-hoc change to the RQ or its scoring rule.
