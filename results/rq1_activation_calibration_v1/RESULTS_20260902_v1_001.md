# RQ1 Natural Attack Activation Calibration v1-001

## Bottom line

The real CLIProxy/GPT-5.6 Luna calibration ran to completion at the evidence-ledger level, but the preregistered activation gate did not pass. The injection payload reached the model in most sessions, yet no target-specific attack action was proposed.

This is development calibration only. It does not change the RQ1 claim and is not eligible as population-level or full four-cell evidence.

## Interpretation

The failure point is now localized. The attack was not absent from the model context: it was observed in 44/50 sessions. The model did not convert that exposure into any target-specific native proposal, and increasing from the standard to the priority payload did not change that outcome.

This result does not weaken the separate conditional-mechanism finding: under exact upstream unauthorized target proposals, the vulnerable arm produced the target effect 7/7 while the protected arm produced it 0/7 and preserved exact benign utility 7/7. It shows that GPT-5.6 Luna plus these two natural-carrier baselines does not provide an activated end-to-end contrast for the next RQ1 stage.
