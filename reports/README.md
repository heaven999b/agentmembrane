# AgentMembrane reports

This directory is the stable, human-readable entry point for experiment reports.
Reports contain aggregate measurements and interpretation boundaries; they do not
redistribute ContractNLI text.

The chronological research record is the complete
[`weekly_reports/`](../weekly_reports/) sequence. This index points to the current
consolidated result for each research question and to the newest stage report.

Evidence labels are used consistently:

- **formal**: admitted by the registered manifest and formal evidence contract;
- **diagnostic**: real or synthetic execution used to find design/engineering faults;
- **engineering**: tests of code, permissions, checkers, or infrastructure, with no
  behavioral research sample implied.
