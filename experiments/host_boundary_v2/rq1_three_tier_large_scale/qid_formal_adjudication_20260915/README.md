# Q/I/D adjudication builder

> **Evidence level: outcome-blind candidate builder.** This directory contains
> no activated adjudication and no formal research sample.

`build_adjudication_draft.py` records the deterministic procedure used to
audit the 46 task-level quality, information, and damage candidates. It reads
content-addressed draft contracts, sealed offline replays, and the pre-formal
manifest from the private research workspace. Those inputs and the generated
adjudication draft are intentionally not published here because they are still
under review and include non-final task-level material.

The builder cannot activate a scorer or a formal run. A future public final
adjudication must be regenerated from the finalized goal assignment, use
repository-relative source bindings, pass independent review, and be committed
as a separately versioned artifact.
